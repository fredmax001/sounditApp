Sound It

Sound It is an entertainment platform for the African and international community in China. Sign in with Google to access your personalised event feed, tickets, artist bookings, and vendor marketplace.

What Sound It does

  • Discover upcoming entertainment events across China
  • Buy and manage tickets for events
  • Book DJs, artists, dancers, and photographers
  • Browse and order from food and product vendors
  • Connect with local businesses via city guides
  • Manage your organiser, artist, or vendor dashboard

Why we use Google Sign-In

Sound It uses Google Sign-In so you can create and access your account quickly and securely. We only request your basic profile (name and email address) to create your Sound It account. We do not access your Google contacts, calendar, Gmail, Drive, or any other Google services.
